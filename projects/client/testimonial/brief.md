## Proposed edit (~60-65 s, 9:16)
1. Hook (0-0.8 s): C2406 smile (0.0-0.6, eased slow-mo) with C2408's audio
   already running under it (J-cut), straight into C2408 in sync. On-screen
   hook line for sound-off viewers (first 3 s), e.g. "صاحب سلسلة ديال محلات
   النظارات كيحكي التجربة ديالو مع SOGIXEL".
2. Intro (C2408, ~12 s), then the founder with a lower third (name · Fondateur,
   Gzenaya Optique).
3. Story (C2410, ~38 s): problem (ROI with other agencies) > Si Saad called >
   store was slow > he gave Saad points to work on > agreed on the return >
   "جاب الله التيسير". Pauses tightened to a natural rhythm; jump cuts hidden
   with alternating wide / closer framings (4K allows ~1.5x crops).

   يخدم مع سي سعد وغيكون satisfait".

   30 secondes" button; SOGIXEL end card.

des points, satisfait), light grade to match the three setups, no SFX
