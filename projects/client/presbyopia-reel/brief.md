hook, shop), C2449 (35.5 s, standing, 3 takes), C2452 (25.9 s, seated, 2 takes).

## Client brief
- Silent hook showing the scroller's pain: he holds the phone farther and
  farther to read. Then the advice and the CTA, smooth and engaging, built to
  loop and retain.
- Brief first; start on the client's word.

## Proposed edit (~22-24 s, loops)
Hook 0-5 s silent + music: close squint -> speed ramp -> wide reveal of the
stretched arm (slow-mo hold) with a growing distance ruler gag; on-screen hook
line. Advice 5-11 s: "مشكل فالقرب" visual (near text blurring, far sharp).
Solution 11-17 s: "أقرب spécialiste" visual (eye exam / calendar).
CTA 17-23 s: share-arrow animation on "تبارطاجي هاد la vidéo"; the last words
"الناس اللي كتباعد التيليفون" cut straight back into the hook -> seamless loop.
Look: smooth zoom transitions on the music's beat, clean karaoke captions
(one line, active word in a navy box), navy/white brand.

## Decisions (client, 2026-10-07)
- Hook text: "حتى نتا كتباعد التيليفون باش تقرا؟"
- No "presbytie" on screen (he does not say it).
- CTA: share (his ask) + save button animation.
